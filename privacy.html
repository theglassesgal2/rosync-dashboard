Privacy Policy

This application ("Bloxsync") requests access to your Roblox account solely for the purpose of verifying your identity on Discord.

Data Collection: We store your Discord User ID, Roblox User ID, and Roblox Username in a secure database.

Data Usage: Your data is used exclusively to update your Discord server nickname and assign roles based on your Roblox group ranks.

Data Removal: You may revoke access or request data deletion at any time by contacting the server administrators.
